This section provides a list of other frameworks to apply deep learning on 
remote sensing and geospatial data.

- [eo-learn](https://github.com/sentinel-hub/eo-learn) (2018)
- [Raster Vision](https://github.com/azavea/raster-vision) (2018)
- [deepforest](https://github.com/weecology/DeepForest) (2019)
- [SITS](https://github.com/e-sensing/sits) (2020)
- [TorchGeo](https://github.com/microsoft/torchgeo) (2021)
- [PaddleRS](https://github.com/PaddlePaddle/PaddleRS) (2022)
- [GeoAI](https://github.com/opengeos/geoai) (2023)
- [segment-geospatial](https://github.com/opengeos/segment-geospatial) (2023)
- [scikit-eo](https://github.com/yotarazona/scikit-eo) (2023)
- [TerraTorch](https://github.com/IBM/terratorch) (2024)
